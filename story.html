Journey 1 of 3 · the customer

Ordering feels like texting a friend.

No app to download. Everything lives inside LINE, the app your customers already open fifty times a day.

Step 1 of 4

Order from a photo menu inside LINE

Customers tap the shop's LINE, browse every drink with real photos, pick sweetness, milk and beans, and choose a pickup day — or ask for a secret cup, or a three-cup omakase set for the week.

Press the button to send one order and watch where it goes.

What the system doesSaves the order, puts it in tomorrow's barista queue, confirms to the customer in LINE, and counts it toward their loyalty card.

Step 2 of 4

Pay with one tap, get reminded politely

When the barista closes the bill, the customer gets a LINE message with the exact amount and a payment link. They send the slip back in the same chat. No chasing in person, no awkward reminders.

What the system doesSends the bill, records the slip, and marks it paid. Unpaid bills show up in the owner's morning summary until they're collected.

Step 3 of 4

A loyalty card they never lose

Every paid cup is stamped automatically. At ten, the customer gets a LINE message and a free cup. The card shows a photo of each drink they had, so it reads like a diary of their coffee.

The shop sets the value of the free cup. If the customer picks something pricier, they pay only the difference — no arguments at the counter.

What the system doesCounts cups, unlocks the reward, tells the barista with a 🎁 on the customer's name, and records the redemption the moment the free cup is made.
Free cup value set to ฿70 in this demo.
Step 4 of 4

The shop remembers them

After a cup, the customer is asked how it tasted. Short "get to know you" questions arrive over time — favourite flavours, birthday, what they'd love to try. If a regular disappears for two weeks, a warm message invites them back. On their birthday, a treat.

What the system doesFeedback, getting-to-know-you, win-back and birthday messages all run on their own. Any batch to more than five people waits for the owner's approval first.

Journey 2 of 3 · the barista

One screen, from first cup to last bill.

Built for a phone on the counter, with wet hands and a queue. Three taps to take an order, one tap to finish a cup.

Step 1 of 3

Today's queue, already sorted

Pre-orders from LINE, walk-ins, Grab orders and office deliveries land in one list. The barista picks the cup or bag, taps Brewed, and moves on.

Tap Brewed on each cup and watch the stock change.

What the system doesDeducts beans by recipe, deducts cups and lids, adds a delivery kit (cooler bag and cup holder) for Grab and office orders, and asks the customer for feedback.
Step 2 of 3

Take an order in three taps

Type two letters of a regular's name. The system shows who has a free cup waiting and offers their usual drink with their usual sweetness.

What the system doesFinds the member, flags rewards, repeats the last order, and applies the shop's pricing rules for the channel.
Step 3 of 3

A lab for the next signature

New drink ideas are tested as real cups, rated, and promoted to the menu when they're ready. When a recipe goes live, a social post draft is written for the owner automatically.

What the system doesTracks every trial, its ingredients and tasting notes, then turns launches into ready-to-edit posts for Facebook and Instagram.

Know where every baht goes.

The owner sees the shop the way a finance team would: targets, trends, channel margins and a full profit-and-loss — from the same numbers, on every screen.

Step 1 of 4

Before opening, the owner gets one LINE message: what to brew today, what's running low, who hasn't come back, and which bills are still open. The time is adjustable.

What the system doesReads orders, stock, customers and payments overnight and writes the brief. A narrated weekly review arrives every Monday.

A day in the life of the system

Most of the work happens before the shop opens and after it closes. Nobody has to remember to do any of it.

  1. 06:00

    Weekly subscription cups are created for the day; the owner's brief goes out.

  2. 07:05

    Win-back and birthday messages are prepared — sent automatically, or held for approval if the list is long.

  3. All day

    Every brewed cup deducts stock, adds packaging and asks its customer for feedback.

  4. At 10 cups

    The customer is told their free cup is ready; the barista sees 🎁 on their name.

  5. 23:59

    Leftover staff cups are tidied so tomorrow's numbers start clean.

  6. Mondays

    A narrated weekly review compares this week with last week.
